Who we are

Clarity from the ground up.

i-GIS Geosystems LTD is a geomatics and spatial consulting firm bridging the gap between physical geography and data-driven decision-making. We combine technical expertise, modern surveying instrumentation and spatial analysis to support infrastructure, land management and environmental sustainability.

Headquartered at Malik Heights, Adams Arcade, Nairobi, our work spans Kenya and the wider East Africa and Horn of Africa region. We serve public utilities, private corporations, conservation groups and international development partners.

Precision Accuracy in every survey and data point.
Innovation Modern GIS and remote sensing technology.
Sustainability Development that respects our environment.

Selected experience

Kenya Power Distribution Network Asset Mapping

Agriculture & land security

Waga & Machame Irrigation Schemes

Topographic surveys, cadastral mapping and GIS integration supporting irrigation design and agricultural land security in Vanga, Kwale County.

International development · WASH

Cross-Border Urban WASH Masterplans

Gender-sensitive, climate-resilient water, sanitation and hygiene masterplans and feasibility studies for Qartho, Dollow and South Galkayo, Somalia. Supported by UNICEF, African Water Facility and AfDB.

⌖
Socio-economic research

Las Anod Water Supply Baseline Survey

Socio-economic baseline surveys and spatial scoping supporting sustainable municipal water delivery in Somaliland, with the Somaliland Development Fund / Earth Water.

🌳
Remote sensing & conservation

Mau Forest Complex Change Detection

Multi-temporal GIS and remote sensing analysis of land cover evolution and human encroachment across 416,542 hectares of the Mau Forest ecosystem.

⛰
Environmental risk assessment

Mandera Soil Erosion & Catchment Risk

Satellite imagery, GIS modelling and RUSLE analysis to estimate land degradation and soil loss across the Mandera Town water catchment.

🌊
Coastal & marine resources

Somalia Seaweed Cultivation Site Scoping

Spatial analytics to identify and map promising marine ecological locations for commercial seaweed cultivation along the Somali coastline.

🌿
Digital conservation mapping

Olerai Conservancy Mapping Initiative

High-precision digital conservation layout mapping ecological boundaries, wildlife infrastructure and land assets.
